## 5. Open questions and conflicts

1. FDA "important protocol deviation" (guidance, R4-036) vs IRB "serious or continuing noncompliance" (binding, R4-028): different concepts and owners. 21 CFR defines neither "serious" nor "continuing"; no source read maps one to the other. Model as two decisions: sponsor/investigator importance classification (guidance), and IRB determination under its written procedures with onward reporting to institutional officials and FDA (binding). Also distinct from EU CTR serious breach (R3).
2. 312.66 "promptly report ... all changes in the research activity" (R4-023) vs FDA-PD24 SIII.B.3 (R4-074): non-important deviations without immediate hazard need not go to the IRB immediately. Whether a deviation is a "change in the research activity" is not settled in 21 CFR; IRB written procedures decide.
3. Immediate-hazard change notification clock (R4-005): 312.30(b)(2)(ii) cross-refers to 56.104(c), which sets 5 working days for emergency-use reporting; applicability of that window to protocol changes is not stated [verify].
4. Site closure: FDA-PD24 "should consider" (R4-071) vs binding 312.56(b) either-or duty once investigator noncompliance is discovered (R4-013). Binding step should rest on 312.56(b).
5. FDA-PD24 is draft guidance: 44 findings carry draft: true and must never be the only basis for a binding step. Its regulatory restatements (312.66, 312.30(b)(2)(ii), 56.108(a)(3)-(4)) were recorded from the CFR text instead.
6. Terminology drift: RBM13 (2013) uses "significant protocol deviations"; FDA-PD24 recommends "important" for all of major/critical/significant (R4-041, R4-087).
7. FDA-PD24 example reporting clock "within x days of occurrence" runs from occurrence, not identification; no FDA day count (R4-054). FDA-PD24 does not mention IND annual reports.
8. RBM13 IV.D.1 vs IV.D.2 placement and page of the sentence on recording/reporting critical deviations differ between fetches (R4-084 low, R4-085 page verify). RBM13 page numbers: fetches mixed printed and PDF numbering; records use TOC printed numbering.
9. Root-cause analysis owner: FDA-PD24 names "the sponsor or clinical investigator" (R4-068); RBM13/RBM23 address sponsors.
10. "updating their analysis" (FDA-PD24 lines 324-325, R4-069): unclear which analysis [verify].
