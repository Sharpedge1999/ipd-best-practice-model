  PASSAGE: 4.7. How can users link the assessment outcome to the notification? When finalising the assessment, Member State (MS) users can link the Ad hoc assessment conclusion to the related notification, triggering its publication on the CTIS Public site as per the revised CTIS transparency rules. To do so, users should access the Notification sub-tab and select the relevant assessment ID available, linking it to the notification (see screenshots below). Alternatively, MS users can directly publish the notification details without linking it to any ad hoc assessment conclusion selecting the option 'no Ad-hoc assessment required'.
