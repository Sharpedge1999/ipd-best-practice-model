If the entire trial is halted (recruitment and/or treatment) a temporary halt notification is submitted; if only parts are halted (e.g. arms, groups, subprotocols) it is reported as an urgent safety measure so that the trial status stays 'ongoing' in CTIS.

  DOCBEST-OUTSIDE 0.66 @ PDF 131 / printed 8: If the entire clinical trial is halted (recruitment and/or treatment), a temporary halt notification should be submitted.
