PARA: Except as provided in 56.109(c), informed consent has to be documented by a written consent form approved by the IRB, signed and dated by the subject or legally authorized representative at the time of consent, with a copy given to the signer. The obliged party is not named in 50.27; 312.60 places the consent duty on the investigator. A missing signature or date is a consent-documentation deviation; FDA-PD24 SIII.A.2 treats a missing initial as a non-important example.
